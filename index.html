ING's gap vs peers: The hub exists (Analytics Office + CAO) but the spokes are still forming. There is no firmwide CDAO with CEO-line authority, no unified self-service data platform, and spoke teams in business units don't yet have dedicated AI headcount on par with DBS or JPM. Transforming to "Data and AI Org" means elevating the hub to CDAO level and maturing spoke teams into business-owned squads with clear shared KPIs.
